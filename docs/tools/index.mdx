## Planned tools

### LLVM IR Visualizer

Inspect generated IR, follow control flow, and connect instructions to source-level intuition.

### Optimization Diff Viewer

Compare outputs across optimization levels, pass pipelines, or GCC versus Clang.

### CFG and SSA Graph Generator

Visualize control-flow graphs, dominance, and SSA relationships.

### Pass Pipeline Explorer

Track how a sequence of passes changes IR over time.

### Benchmark Artifact Explorer

Browse benchmark JSON, CSV, and comparison outputs with enough structure to spot real differences.
