# 3.4 OpenMP project

## What you'll learn

- The serial sum and the OpenMP sum print the same number.
- You keep both times.

## Video

The recording for this page is not up yet. These notes are the script for that recording and for the short slide deck.

## Concept

Build the serial loop and the reduction loop in one file. Print both totals and both times. The totals match. The times are whatever your machine reports. Write those numbers down. Later chapters have to beat a real serial baseline, not a guessed one.

## Picture

```text
serial total, parallel total, two times.
```

## Hands-on

Use the six-number list, then a longer list you fill with a simple pattern, so the parallel region has work to do. Keep the printed totals equal.

## What changed the time

Record the two times from your run. Do not copy a time from a book.
